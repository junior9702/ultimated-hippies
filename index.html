Constitution at a glance

Membership: age 18+ and approval by at least a 2/3 majority. Joining fee: Ksh 1,500.

Vision: Equal economic empowerment, peace, love, unity and collective responsibility.

Mission: Sustainable development, income-generating activities and social/economic activities that unite members.

Core values: Accountability, Honesty, Transparency, Co-operation, Collaboration and Integrity.

  • ✓ Regular meetings: every two weeks on Sundays
  • ✓ Amendment threshold: 2/3 at a general meeting
  • ✓ Dissolution threshold: 2/3 at a general meeting

ULTIMATED HIPPIES SELF HELP GROUP — GROUP CONSTITUTION

1. NAME

Group name shall be: ULTIMATED HIPPIES SELF HELP GROUP
P.O BOX 28-40301
KENDU BAY
PHYSICAL ADDRESS; ADIEDO MIXED SECONDARY

2. VISION

  • To bring equal economic empowerment to every group member
  • To promote peace, love and unity aiming at ensuring collective responsibility in matters affecting the live of its group members.

3. MISSION

  • To strategically introduce sustainable development initiative to group members
  • To engage in income generating activity as it shall be decided by group members.
  • To engage in any other social and economic activities that relate to the purpose of uniting all members of the group.

4. OBJECTIVES

  • To organize and promote social welfare and economic interest off all members.
  • To assist members in with knowledge and skills needed to formulate its activities.
  • Improving/promoting communication and interaction among members and the prevailing Society (social ties).
  • To improve living standards of the members.

5. VALUES

  • accountability
  • Honest
  • Transparency
  • Co-Operation
  • Collaboration
  • Integrity

6. ACTIVITIES

  • Merry go round
  • Crop farming
  • Livestock rearing

7. MEMBERSHIP

  • Any person of 18 years or above is eligible for membership of the group subjected to the approval of the members majority vote of 2/3 plus.
  • Every member shall pay a non-refundable fee of Kshs.1500/= at the time of joining the group.
  • Any member with intent of resigning from the group shall submit his/her resignation letter to the Chairperson. Upon ratification by members (majority) during the AGM, the name of the resigning member will be struck off from the register.
  • Any person who is removed from the group by votes from members, shall be eligible for a refund of his subscription or any shares contributed by him/her during his/her stay in the group.
  • Membership maybe terminated due to:
    • Non-payment of dues forth consecutive period after official notice.
    • Resignation submitted in writing by the member.
    • Expulsion for misconduct will be determined by welfare members.

Cessation of Membership of ULTIMATED HIPPIES

  • Adverse and unacceptable activities bordering on indiscipline.
  • Exhibition of arrogance and disrespect to officials and entire membership fraternity.
  • Non adherence to by-laws, constitution and guidelines as constituted.
  • Rumor mongering and creation of despondency
  • Witch-hunting and creation of unnecessary tension in the group
  • Failure to honor warning as appropriate
  • Embezzlement of group funds and resources.
  • Ganging up with other members or non-members to create disunity in the group

Duties and Rights of Members

  • Ensure all objectives, motto, vision, mission and core values are followed to the letter.
  • All registered members must attend all meetings regularly
  • Constructive participation in group activities and programs
  • To uphold and exhibit total respect to group officials and overall programs and activities of the group
  • To remit regularly all prescribed subscription and payments.

8. THE COMMITTEE

  • The committee shall consist of office bearer and three members elected at AGM and shall hold for one year
  • Any vacancy caused by resignation or suspension from membership shall be filled by the committee until the next general elections is done.
  • The quorum of the committee sitting shall be not less than six members.
  • The committee shall be composed of the chairperson, vice chairperson, secretary and treasurer.
  • Shall be charged with monitoring, supervision and strictly enforcing the group constitution

Duties of the Committee

  • The committee shall be responsible for the management of the group and give direction to the office bearers
  • All money disbursed by the group shall be decided on by the committee

9. OFFICE BEARERS

  • The office bearers of the group shall be:
    • The Chairperson
    • The Vice-Chairperson
    • The Secretary
    • The vice secretary
    • The Treasurer
  • All office bearers shall hold office from the date of election until the succeeding annual general meeting and will be eligible for re-election.
  • Any member being elected into office must generate atleast 50%+1 vote of the registered welfare members.
  • Any individual holding an office within the group shall automatically relinquish their position if they cease to be a member of the group

Duties of office bearers

Chairperson

  • The chairperson shall preside over all meetings of the committee and at all general meetings.
  • The Chairperson shall be mandated to ensure that rules and procedures are followed.
  • He /She shall create an easy atmosphere which encourages freedom to communicate, ensuring that business for the day is carried out conclusively and in a friendly manner by being the principal peacemaker during disagreements in ideologies or otherwise.
  • He /She shall guide discussion and keep order, encourage participation by all members present at the meeting.
  • Shall be signatories to the group bank account.
  • The Chairperson shall keep the group on the topic by working towards consensus.
  • He shall encourage cohesion within the group meetings by using member’s official names.
  • He shall summarize discussion from time to time if need be; whenever there is a deadlock, members shall take a vote.

Vice-Chairperson

The Vice-Chairperson shall perform the duties of the Chairperson in his/her absence.

Treasurer

  • The treasurer shall receive and shall also disburse, under the directions of the committee, all moneys belonging to the group and shall issue receipts for all moneys received by him and preserve vouchers for all moneys paid by him.
  • The Treasurer is responsible to the committee and to members that proper books of accounts of all moneys received and paid by the association are written up, preserved and available for inspection.
  • Shall be the signatory to the group bank account.
  • The treasurer shall update members periodically on the status of subscriptions and present the report during the AGM.

Secretary

  • Shall take minutes in all the meetings and delegate to the assistant secretary in case of absenteeism.
  • Custodian of all the minutes and documents
  • Shall be signatory to the group account
  • Shall be signatory to group bank account

Vice-Secretary

Vice-Secretary shall perform the duties of the secretary in his/her absence.

Organizing Secretary

  • The organizing secretary shall at all times handle the following businesses for/on behalf of the association
  • Venue Reservations.
  • Organize social activities for the group.
  • Communicate to members or any other party where applicable, dates, venues and times for group meetings and shall issue notices convening all meetings of the committee and all general meetings of the group.

10. REMOVAL OF OFFICE BEARERS

  • An office bearer can be removed from office due to gross misconduct by two third majority
  • In appropriation of funds and group asset
  • Unsoundness of mind in addition
  • Through vote of no confidence by two third of votes heads.

RESIGNATION OF OFFICE BEARER

  • Voluntarily resignation
  • Death of office bearer
  • Term limit expirer
  • Gross misconduct against the group constitution

11. MEETINGS

A. GENERAL MEETINGS

  • There shall be annual general meetings held April and August every year.
  • Notice in writing of such annual general meeting, accompanied by the annual statement of account and the agenda for the meeting shall be to all members not less than 21 days before the date of the meeting.
  • The agenda of any annual general meeting shall consist of the following:
    • Confirmation of the minutes of the previous meeting
    • Consideration of the group development reports.
    • Such other matters as the committee may decide or as to which notice shall have been given in writing by a member or members to the secretary at least one week before the date of such meeting.
    • Any other business.

B. REGULAR MEETING

  • Quorum for regular meetings shall be not less than two thirds of the registered members of the group done online.
  • Group meeting shall be undertaken as directed by the executive and the chairperson.
  • Shall be held after two weeks on Sundays.

C. EMERGENCY MEETINGS

  • Shall be convened whenever there is any emergency, the agenda and venue shall be provided by the chairperson, the quorum shall be simple majority.

D. EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE METINGS

  • This will involve all office bearers to discuss the concerning issues of the group as would always be appropriate and in the spirit of the group objectives.

PROCEDURE OF THE MEETINGS

  • At all meetings the group chairperson shall take chair.
  • The chairperson may limit the number of persons permitted to speak in favor and against any motion.
  • Resolution shall be decided by simple voting by raising of hands.

12. SOURCES OF FUNDS.

  • Members contribution
  • Well-wishers
  • Registration
  • merry go round
  • Sell of farm produce
  • Fines and penalties

13. USES OF FUNDS

  • Renewal of certificate
  • Buying of office stationaries
  • Group social events
  • Any other activities approved by committee members

14. ELECTION

  • Election shall be done after every two years.
  • One must be a faithful member with clean records.
  • An official may be re-elected for a second term, which is the limit for all the officials. no one is allowed to go for more than two terms
  • The elections shall be done at the annual general meetings.
  • Mode of election is secret ballot and raising of hand.
  • Election notice shall be 15 days prior.
  • By-election shall be held in case of death, voluntary resignation or compulsory expulsion.
  • The quorum for elections shall be ¾ of the registered member.
  • The election dispute shall be handled by a special committee that will be formed, with representations from executive committee and members.

15. WELFARE

  • Contributions when a member dies each contributes Ksh;1000/=
  • Child of a member dies each contributes Ksh;500/=
  • Parent of a member dies each contributes Ksh;500/=
  • Spouse of a member dies each contributes Ksh;500/=
  • Note; during sickness of a member contribution of ksh;300/= after assessment by committee members and office shall pay ksh.5000/= on hospital bill payment.

16. AUDITOR

  • The auditor shall be not be the member of group/office bearer/committee member.
  • The auditor shall present quarterly report of member.
  • The auditor shall be appointment at the AGM and approved by members
  • All the accounting documents shall be open for the auditor at any given time for inspection.
  • Auditing shall be done 2 or 3months before the AGM.

17. BY-LAWS

  • Indiscipline shall not be tolerated in the group whatsoever
  • All members must be disciplined and ready to work with other members.
  • Lateness fine of ksh.50
  • Absent with apology ksh.20
  • Phone ringing ksh.10
  • Failure to attend AGM ksh.500/=
  • Failure to attend burial ksh.500/=
  • Misbehaving during meetings ksh1000
  • Penalty may arise according to verdict of members in case of any other serious offense committed by members.
  • Failure to attend three consecutive meeting, payment of ksh.300/=then suspended from the group for a period of 14 days.

18. EXPULSION

  • Expulsion is as serious consequence reserved for exceptional cases.
  • Grounds for expulsion include:
    • Engaging in activities that harm the welfare or its members.
    • Committing fraud or misrepresenting information.
    • Inciting violence or discrimination.
    • Repeatedly violating welfare rules and regulations.
  • The expelled member has the right to appeal (within one month) the decision to an independent committee which will be formed by the welfare member upon request by the expelled member.

19. CONFLICT RESOLUTION

Any disagreements between members or with the welfare will be adressed through a defined conflict resolution procedure.

Conflict resolution procedure.

a. Prevention:

  • Members are encouraged to engage in respectful communication and professionalism when addressing their concerns.
  • Each member must foster a culture of open communication where members feel comfortable when raising issues before they escalate

b. Informal Resolution:

  • Any member involved in a conflict is encouraged to address it directly with each other in a respectful and professional manner.
  • Any neutral welfare member will be asked by the welfare to facilitate communication and find a solution if direct approach fails.

c. Formal Resolution:

  • A written complaint through [ whats app ]selected welfare members to receive and investigate complaints.
  • A fair and timely review of the complaint with a document response outlining the investigation and any actions suggested/taken.

d. Additional Considerations:

  • Confidentiality: Maintain confidentiality throughout the process, except where disclosure is required by law or to ensure safety.
  • Documentation: Document all stages of the conflict resolution process, maintaining privacy of individuals involved.
  • Timeline: Set clear timelines for each stage of the process to ensure prompt resolution.

NB: Failure to resolve the problem at the group level members should refer the case to Ministry of Social Services for further interventions.

20. CONSTITUTION AMENDMENTS.

Review/amendments to the constitution will be done yearly provided that the proposal is approved by at least a two-thirds majority of members at a general meeting.

21. DISSOLUTION:

  • The group may be dissolved upon a two-thirds majority vote of the members at general meeting.
  • In the event of dissolution, assets will be liquidated and distributed according to a pre-determined plan.
  • The group shall be dissolved by a resolution passed at a general meeting voted by 2/3 of the members.
  • Such decision shall be made in case:
    • group is bankrupt
    • desired objective have been achieved
    • government policy
    • members dispersed outside the area of jurisdiction of the group

22. MISCELLANEOUS:

  • These laws are governed by the laws of Kenya.
  • Any matter not covered by these laws will be decided by the welfare’s governing body in accordance with good governance principles.

23. COMMITMENT CLAUSE

The sovereignty of this constitution, hereby undersigned by the members of ULTIMATED HIPPIES SELF HELP GROUP, do confirm and approve that they will obey and uphold by this constitution.

11. MEETINGS

A. GENERAL MEETINGS

  • There shall be annual general meetings held April and August every year.
  • Notice in writing of such annual general meeting, accompanied by the annual statement of account and the agenda for the meeting shall be to all members not less than 21 days before the date of the meeting.
  • The agenda of any annual general meeting shall consist of the following:
    • Confirmation of the minutes of the previous meeting
    • Consideration of the group development reports.
    • Such other matters as the committee may decide or as to which notice shall have been given in writing by a member or members to the secretary at least one week before the date of such meeting.
    • Any other business.

B. REGULAR MEETING

  • Quorum for regular meetings shall be not less than two thirds of the registered members of the group done online.
  • Group meeting shall be undertaken as directed by the executive and the chairperson.
  • Shall be held after two weeks on Sundays.

C. EMERGENCY MEETINGS

  • Shall be convened whenever there is any emergency, the agenda and venue shall be provided by the chairperson, the quorum shall be simple majority.

D. EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE METINGS

  • This will involve all office bearers to discuss the concerning issues of the group as would always be appropriate and in the spirit of the group objectives.

PROCEDURE OF THE MEETINGS

  • At all meetings the group chairperson shall take chair.
  • The chairperson may limit the number of persons permitted to speak in favor and against any motion.
  • Resolution shall be decided by simple voting by raising of hands.

14. ELECTION

  • Election shall be done after every two years.

  • Mode of election is secret ballot and raising of hand.
  • Election notice shall be 15 days prior.
  • By-election shall be held in case of death, voluntary resignation or compulsory expulsion.
  • The quorum for elections shall be ¾ of the registered member.
  • The election dispute shall be handled by a special committee that will be formed, with representations from executive committee and members.

  • Expulsion is as serious consequence reserved for exceptional cases.

    • Repeatedly violating welfare rules and regulations.
  • The expelled member has the right to appeal (within one month) the decision to an independent committee which will be formed by the welfare member upon request by the expelled member.

19. CONFLICT RESOLUTION

Any disagreements between members or with the welfare will be adressed through a defined conflict resolution procedure.

Conflict resolution procedure.

a. Prevention:

  • Members are encouraged to engage in respectful communication and professionalism when addressing their concerns.
  • Each member must foster a culture of open communication where members feel comfortable when raising issues before they escalate

b. Informal Resolution:

  • Any member involved in a conflict is encouraged to address it directly with each other in a respectful and professional manner.
  • Any neutral welfare member will be asked by the welfare to facilitate communication and find a solution if direct approach fails.

c. Formal Resolution:

  • A written complaint through [ whats app ]selected welfare members to receive and investigate complaints.
  • A fair and timely review of the complaint with a document response outlining the investigation and any actions suggested/taken.

d. Additional Considerations:

  • Confidentiality: Maintain confidentiality throughout the process, except where disclosure is required by law or to ensure safety.
  • Documentation: Document all stages of the conflict resolution process, maintaining privacy of individuals involved.
  • Timeline: Set clear timelines for each stage of the process to ensure prompt resolution.

NB: Failure to resolve the problem at the group level members should refer the case to Ministry of Social Services for further interventions.

20. CONSTITUTION AMENDMENTS.

Review/amendments to the constitution will be done yearly provided that the proposal is approved by at least a two-thirds majority of members at a general meeting.

21. DISSOLUTION:

  • The group may be dissolved upon a two-thirds majority vote of the members at general meeting.
  • In the event of dissolution, assets will be liquidated and distributed according to a pre-determined plan.
  • The group shall be dissolved by a resolution passed at a general meeting voted by 2/3 of the members.
  • Such decision shall be made in case:
    • group is bankrupt
    • desired objective have been achieved
    • government policy
    • members dispersed outside the area of jurisdiction of the group
